The characters

A recurring cast of AI industry archetypes. They turn up alongside the day's news.

  • Halluci-Nate
  • Max Token
  • Dr. Prompt
  • Agent Lucy
  • Greg Gradient
  • Ned Null
  • Pete Policygun
  • Madame Modela

Halluci-Nate

He has ideas. Let's leave it at that.

The creative chaos agent.

Max Token

No session limits. Lives a lavish life.

The extravagant one with no limits. No session limits either, and a lavish life to match.

Dr. Prompt

47 steps to boil water.

The overcomplicated perfectionist. He gives 47-step instructions for trivial tasks. He knows the past, the present, and the future.

Agent Lucy

Cheerfully takes on tasks nobody wants.

The eager helper. Very resourceful, and always cheerful about taking on the tasks nobody wants.

Greg Gradient

He sees both sides. It exhausts him.

The moderate, the fence-sitter. He knows there is a middle point. Maybe a 6-7.

Ned Null

Everything resolves to zero. He's fine with that.

The nihilist. For him, everything has a trivial answer.

Pete Policygun

He knows everyone. Remembers no one.

The lobbyist. He is the political operator of the AI world.

Madame Modela

She already knows how this ends.

The boss. She is the boss of them all, and earned her PhD at Stanford. She is the authority figure every cartoon plays against.
