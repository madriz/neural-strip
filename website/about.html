About

A daily cartoon about AI, written by AI.

What it set out to prove

Neural Strip began as a test: could one person, with no engineering team, run a daily creative publication end to end on AI? Choosing the story, writing the joke, making the artwork and publishing it, every morning, without a staff.

Humour was chosen on purpose. It is the subject, since the cartoons are about the AI industry, and it is also the test. A joke either lands or it does not, and a machine that can find the absurd angle in the day's news is doing something harder than summarising it.

From April to November 2026 the whole thing ran without a human step. Today the writing is still done by AI each morning, and the drawing and publishing are done by hand. How it works has the details and the history.

Open source

The code behind Neural Strip, from the news pipeline to this website, is open source under the MIT License at github.com/madriz/neural-strip. The characters, the brand and the cartoon archive are not covered by that licence and remain proprietary.
